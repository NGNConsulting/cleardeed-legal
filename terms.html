ClearDeed is a planning and educational calculator. It compares ways of paying off a mortgage — including paying as scheduled, extra principal, biweekly payments, and "velocity banking" with a home equity line of credit (HELOC) — using only the numbers you enter. It does not connect to your bank, lender or any financial account, and it never moves money.
