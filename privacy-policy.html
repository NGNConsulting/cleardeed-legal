ClearDeed — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

ClearDeed ("the app") is a personal mortgage-payoff planning and tracking tool published by NGN Consulting Corp. This policy explains what the app does, and does not, do with your information.

What we collect

Nothing is sent to us. ClearDeed does not have user accounts, does not require sign-up, and does not connect to your bank or any financial institution. Every number you enter — your mortgage balance, HELOC details, income, and bills — is stored only in a local database on your own phone. NGN Consulting Corp cannot see, access, or receive any of this information, because the app never transmits it. If you have Android's device backup turned on, Android may include the app's data in your own Google account backup, the same as other apps on your phone; that copy is controlled by you and Google, not by us.

Cookies and tracking

ClearDeed does not use cookies, advertising identifiers, analytics, or any other tracking technology — in the app or on this website.

Backups you create

ClearDeed can save your plan and your Monthly True-Up history to a backup file, only when you ask it to. You choose where that file goes (for example your own Google Drive, an email to yourself, or a folder on your phone), using your phone’s standard share screen. ClearDeed never uploads backups anywhere and has no copy of them. The file is not encrypted and contains the numbers you entered, so keep it somewhere private. Restoring a backup replaces the plan on the phone you restore it to.

Subscriptions and payment

ClearDeed offers an optional one-time purchase and an optional yearly subscription, processed entirely through Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment details — that is handled by Google under its own privacy policy. ClearDeed only stores, locally on your device, which of its purchases you have (the one-time Full analysis, an active Tracking subscription, or neither).

Third parties

The only third-party service involved in using ClearDeed is Google Play Billing, for processing optional purchases. ClearDeed contains no advertising, no analytics software, and no crash-reporting service as of this version.

Data retention and deletion

We keep no copy of your data, so there is nothing for you to ask us to delete. Uninstalling ClearDeed deletes its data from your phone; if you use Android's device backup, you can delete any backed-up copy from your Google account settings.

Children's privacy

ClearDeed is not directed at children and is not designed for use by anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children, and in any case the app collects no information from anyone at all, as described above.

Not financial advice

For planning and educational use only, not a guarantee. It never connects to your bank or moves money. ClearDeed is not a lender, bank, broker, or financial advisor and does not provide financial, investment, or tax advice. Every projection it shows is an estimate calculated from the numbers you enter, not a guarantee of any outcome.
